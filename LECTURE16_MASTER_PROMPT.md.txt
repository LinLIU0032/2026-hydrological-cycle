# 23. 核心课程思想

本讲不能设计成：

Part 1 Water Cycle
+
Part 2 Carbon Cycle
+
结束

需要形成统一 Earth System narrative。

主线：

**RESERVOIRS**
↓
**FLUXES**
↓
**RESIDENCE TIME / TIMESCALES**
↓
**COUPLING**
↓
**FEEDBACKS**

学生最终需要能够回答：

### Question 1

Where is Earth's water stored, and how does water move among reservoirs?

地球上的水储在哪里，又如何在不同储库之间迁移？

### Question 2

Where is carbon stored, and how does carbon move through the Earth system?

碳储在哪里，又如何在地球系统中循环？

### Question 3

How do atmosphere, oceans, rocks, and life interact?

大气、海洋、岩石和生命如何相互作用？

### Question 4

How are the water cycle and carbon cycle coupled?

水循环与碳循环如何彼此耦合？

### Question 5

How do feedback mechanisms regulate Earth's climate?

反馈机制如何放大或抑制气候变化？
